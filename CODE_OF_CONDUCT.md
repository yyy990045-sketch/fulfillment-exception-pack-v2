# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Be respectful, receive feedback constructively, and focus on what is best for the project and its users. Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and publishing private information without permission are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the repository owner through the repository's private channel. Maintainers may remove contributions or restrict participation when necessary to protect participants.

This document is adapted from the Contributor Covenant; the project has not copied a separate enforcement contact or invented a public address.
