# Visual Design Rebuild

## Goal

Raise the existing product from an internal-admin visual style to a mature B2B enterprise product without changing the information architecture or Backend behavior.

## Scope

- Unified color, radius, spacing, typography, surface, Badge, Button, input, and page-width tokens.
- Compact App Shell and navigation states.
- Context Digital Thread and exception list redesign.
- Record Header, sticky subnavigation, evidence split view, analysis, decision, and governance visual hierarchy.
- Operations summary/table refinements.
- Solution Pack three-level workspace and Chinese presentation mapping.
- Before/After screenshots at 1440x900 and 1600x900 through EGo browser.

## Non-Goals

- No information architecture changes.
- No new pages or features.
- No Backend business changes.
- No brand-specific visual copying.

## Acceptance

- Pages share one coherent modern enterprise visual language.
- Analysis and Decision body widths are constrained.
- Context and Record pages clearly separate list, record, facts, analysis, decision, and governance.
- No horizontal overflow at required desktop sizes.
- Frontend, E2E, backend, typecheck, eval, and OpenAPI checks pass.
