# Commercial Delivery Polish

## Goal

Move the existing product from a mature internal system to a customer-demonstrable pilot without adding pages or business capabilities.

## Scope

- Remove engineering terminology from primary business UI.
- Format primary-layer times consistently.
- Add confirmation Sheets for approve, execute, and close.
- Unify Header and governance mutation state through one page-level controller.
- Add lightweight success feedback.
- Separate product brand from module navigation.
- Professionalize demo environment wording.
- Compress governance explanations and business-outcome language.
- Record unresolved Backend contracts in the frontend backlog.

## Acceptance

- Real browser flow covers Operations, Manufacturing, Evidence, Analysis, Decision, Governance, Operations, Solution Pack, and Finance Generic Workspace.
- High-impact actions require confirmation and duplicate execution is prevented.
- Header and governance workspace share pending, error, success, and capability refresh state.
- Frontend, E2E, backend, typecheck, eval, and OpenAPI checks pass.
